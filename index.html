Good to know before you come in

First time colouring with us

Pop in for a quick patch test at least 48 hours before. It takes two minutes and no appointment.

Wear your hair as usual

For cuts, come with it dry and styled the way you normally wear it, so we can see how it sits.

Running late

Text us. Past 15 minutes we may have to shorten the service or move you to a later chair.

Cancelling

A day's notice by text, please. There's no deposit to book.
